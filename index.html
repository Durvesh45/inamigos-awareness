01 / WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organisation founded on 23 September 2020 and based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. The Foundation works towards social welfare through community-focused initiatives.

Its work includes supporting education, providing food and essentials, empowering women, caring for animals, protecting the environment and helping young people build useful skills.

02 / OUR INITIATIVES

Projects that create positive impact

Each initiative focuses on a real community need and encourages people to take part in lasting change.

EDUCATION

Project Bachpanshala

Supports learning and educational opportunities for children from underserved communities.

Purpose: encourage learning and help children build a brighter future.

FOOD & ESSENTIALS

Project SEVA

Food and clothing support activities help people and families facing difficult circumstances.

Purpose: promote food security, care and dignity.

WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Project Udaan

Encourages women's confidence, skill development and opportunities for greater independence.

Purpose: support equality and self-reliance.

ANIMAL WELFARE

Project Jeev

Promotes kindness and care for animals, including support for stray and vulnerable animals.

Purpose: encourage compassion for every living being.

ENVIRONMENT

Project Prakriti

Promotes tree plantation, environmental awareness and sustainable community practices.

Purpose: help create cleaner, greener surroundings.

SKILL DEVELOPMENT

Project Vikas

Offers learning and internship opportunities that help young people develop practical and professional skills.

Purpose: build confidence, experience and employability.
